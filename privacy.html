Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mount Parche collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our online shop and ordering system.

Mount Parche sells clothes, accessories, crockery, and other items through this online shop.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfil an order, we collect the following necessary information:

  • Name: So we know who the order is for.
  • Phone Number: To contact you regarding your order status, delivery, or if we have questions about your items.
  • Delivery Address: Used strictly to ship your items to you.
  • Order Details: The items you selected and any special instructions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for order fulfillment. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketing or advertising agencies. We use your data to:

  • Process and fulfil your order.
  • Send order details to Mount Parche staff through secure operational tools.
  • Deliver your items to the provided address.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your order details are securely transmitted to Mount Parche staff to fulfil your order. Your cart items are temporarily saved locally on your device. Payment details are processed by Razorpay; Mount Parche does not store card or UPI credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Vercel for hosting, Upstash Redis for operational data, and Razorpay for online payments. These services are governed by their own privacy and security policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, email mountparche@duck.com.
